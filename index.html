About Us

At just 22 years old, I'm passionate about delivering top-tier car detailing services that go beyond a simple wash. I founded my business to offer premium detailing with meticulous attention to every detail, ensuring your vehicle looks and feels brand new. Whether it’s an exterior polish or a full interior deep clean, my goal is to provide an exceptional experience that leaves your car shining with perfection. I take pride in bringing out the best in every vehicle I work on, treating each one as if it were my own.

  • Premium Quality
  • Personalized Service
  • Professional Care
  • Passionate Expertise

Our team is a group of dedicated professionals who share a passion for cars and a commitment to excellence. Each member brings a unique set of skills and expertise, ensuring that every vehicle we detail receives the highest level of care.

We work collaboratively to provide a seamless and efficient service, from the moment you book with us to the final reveal of your perfectly detailed car. United by a shared love for the art of detailing, we pride ourselves on delivering outstanding results that exceed your expectations every time.

Services

Level 1

This level is for cars that are regularly maintained or that are not in need of a more heavy duty cleaning. $120 + $10 gas fee

Level 2

This level is for cars that need a little more love than level 1 and are in need of a more focused detail on certain areas. $150 + $10 gas fee

Level 3

This level is for cars that need an extreme amount of cleaning all throughout the car and have no specific areas of cleaning but rather the whole car just needs a full makeover. $180 + $10 gas fee

Level 4

This level would be for any cars that have any of the following. Throw up, extreme amounts of pet hair and or pet accidents in the car. This level would also apply to any extreme stains and or food remains. $220 + $10 gas fee
